Objective: Conquer planet through pollution expansion while defending against native resistance

  **Resource System (6 Resources)**
  - Energy (powers buildings)
  - Iron (basic material)
  - Copper (advanced material)
  - Steel (refined from Iron)
  - Robotic Swarm (for Worker production)
  - Mana Crystal (from enemy drops)

  **Building System**
  - Command: Base (game over if destroyed)
  - Energy Generators: 5 tiers (Steam → Diesel → Nuclear → Fusion + Mana variant)
  - Extractors: Iron, Copper, Mana (3 tiers each)
  - Production: Steel Foundry, 4 Worker Factories, Recycling Plant
  - Defense: 3 wall tiers, 4 turret types, Fortress
  - Research: Laboratory

 ** Worker System (4 Types)**
  - Builders - Construct buildings
  - Extractors - Operate extractors & generators
  - Researchers - Operate Research Lab
  - Drones - Man turrets (energy-independent)

 ** Technology Tree**
  - 5 tiers of research
  - Building unlocks, efficiency upgrades, worker improvements
  - Two unlock methods: Researchable vs Mission Rewards

**Enemy System (13 Types, 4 Races)**
  - Humans (7): Peasants, Soldiers, Archers, 4 Elemental Mages
  - Dwarves (2): Tunnelers (bypass Iron Walls), Warriors (high HP tanks)
  - Demons (2): Flying (bypass walls), Ground (AOE magic)
  - Elves (2): Healers (drain energy), Stunners (disable turrets)

  **Combat Mechanics**
  - Tower defense style (only buildings attack)
  - Turret modes: Manned (0 E/s, Drone-operated) vs Automated (8-20 E/s)
  - Enemy targeting priority: Base → Generators → Extractors → Defenses → Walls
  - 7 damage types: Physical, Fire, Water, Earth, Air, Dark, Light

  **Pollution System (Dual Effect)**
  - Permanent: Dead vegetation stays dead, creates buildable land
  - Reversible: Scales enemy difficulty (spawn rate, wave size, stats)
  - 5 pollution tiers (0-250, 250-500, 500-750, 750-1000, 1000+)

 ** Progression Systems**
  - Chapter-based: 5 chapters, 10 missions each
  - Mission continuity: Progress carries within chapter, fresh start between chapters
  - Objectives: Main (required) + Optional (bonus rewards)
  - Mission types: Survive Waves, Kill Count, Resource Goal, Research Under Fire, Reach Pollution

  **Special Mechanics**
  - Ore Mound System: Fixed locations, one extractor per mound, infinite resources
  - Energy Crisis: If Energy = 0, most buildings stop (except manned turrets)
  - Wall Tiers: Iron → Steel (stops tunnelers) → Null-Magic (80% magic resist)
  - Building Upgrades: In-place upgrades (costs resources + builders)
  - Worker Assignment: Reassign anytime, killed if building destroyed

**Boss Fights (10 Total)**
  - Human Warlord (Ch1)
  - Deep King (Ch2)
  - Demon Prince (Ch3)
  - Elven Queen (Ch4)
  - Four Warlords + Ancient Guardian (Ch5)

 ** UI Systems**
  - Main Menu (New Game, Load Game, Options, Credits)
  - Save/Load System (3 save slots per profile)
  - Settings (Graphics, Audio, Gameplay, Controls)
  - Mission briefing/objectives
  - Resource HUD

 ** Meta Features**
  - Mission rewards (tech unlocks, building unlocks, resources)
  - Technology persistence (carries between chapters)
  - Difficulty scaling (pollution-based)
  - Optional objectives (mastery challenges)
