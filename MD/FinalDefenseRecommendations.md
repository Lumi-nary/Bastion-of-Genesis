## Panelist 1

- The first part should be divided into multiple bite-sized introductions to the mechanics.
- The tutorial needs to be more direct and "in your face" because the goal is to make the game more accessible to non-RTS players.
- Copy the tutorial approach used by more accessible RTS games.
- Balance the difficulty. It is too hard if the player has no knowledge about strategies or blueprints.
- Add animations and visual feedback.
- Simple mechanics do not automatically mean a lower barrier of entry.
- RTS games can overwhelm players by presenting many things at once.
- Use visual novel storytelling to casually explain mechanics and serve as the tutorial.
- Highlight mechanics and explain them.

## Panelist 2

- The tutorial is not fully implemented as intended or as described in the paper.
- Add animations and visual feedback.
- Some UI elements are not placed correctly.
